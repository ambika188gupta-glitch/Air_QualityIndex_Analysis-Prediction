# 🌍 Air Quality Index (AQI) Analysis and Prediction

An end-to-end Data Analysis and Machine Learning project focused on analyzing air pollution levels, identifying pollution hotspots, and predicting air quality to gain meaningful environmental insights.

## 📌 Project Overview

Air pollution is one of the major environmental challenges affecting human health and quality of life. This project analyzes air quality data to understand pollution patterns across different cities and states and explores machine learning techniques for AQI prediction.

The project focuses on identifying major pollutants, analyzing geographical pollution trends, and understanding the factors influencing air quality.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze city-wise and state-wise air quality trends.
- Identify the most and least polluted cities.
- Analyze the distribution of major air pollutants.
- Understand relationships between different pollutants.
- Identify pollution hotspots.
- Apply machine learning techniques for AQI prediction.
